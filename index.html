Reimagining the definition of luxury automotive surface engineering. Starting from iconic shapes of Italian and European automotive heritage, our vision is to create from the ground up the most advanced paint correction, PPF, and ceramic preservation studio in the world.

Authentic signature.

A futuristic project

The goal of Cardose Automobili isn't just to apply standard protection in the market. Our mission is restoring and shielding automobiles from scratch, emerging as a world-wide reference point in the fields of high-end surface engineering and bespoke detailing.

The Cardose GT Series programs have been engineered in materials and chemistry to give carbon-fiber and aluminum supercars a smooth, liquid-glass, and timeless finish. Every detail of Veloce T12, GT Electric, GT Super, and Sport Prototipo preserves the original factory soul while adding an invisible modern armor.

Starting from 120-point ultrasonic clear-coat depth mapping and custom-extended 3D CAD templates, every external panel is perfected inside positively pressurized HEPA cleanrooms. It's beautiful.

Driving matters.

The iconic soul

The surface research carried out by Cardose preserves the original soul of an automotive icon while standing out for the excellent craftsmanship and cleanroom processes behind the protection of each single component: even those ones you'll never see.

Whether safeguarding a V12 collector masterpiece, a daily grand tourer, or a track-focused GT3 RS, Cardose combines artisanal hand-finishing with aerospace polymer technology.

The Italian art of the automobile, reimagined.

A new coachbuilt era

The Cardose Veloce T12 Series marks the beginning of a new chapter in surgical multi-stage paint correction and restorative surface detailing. Built upon our proprietary four-stage jeweling protocol, the T12 program is conceived for collectors who demand distortion-free mirror clarity.

One technical foundation. Different proportions, identities and interpretations. Each unmistakably Cardose.

A technical canvas.

Engineering freedom

Before a single rotary or dual-action polisher touches the bodywork, our Defelsko PosiTector ultrasonic scanner logs clear-coat thickness to 0.1 microns across every carbon-fibre, aluminum, and steel panel.

Under Scangrip CRI 96+ spectral inspection tunnels, 99.8% of swirl marks, holograms, acid etching, and factory orange peel are permanently leveled without fillers or glazes.

Coachbuilt, once again.

One architecture. Many identities.

During the 1950s and 1960s, the great Italian coach-builders transformed shared mechanical foundations into profoundly different automobiles. Cardose Veloce T12 reinterprets this artisanal dedication by tailoring custom pad, wool, and sub-micron diminishing abrasive combinations to each vehicle's specific lacquer hardness.

Beauty is the purpose. Engineering is the method.

Designed without compromise.

Every line is refined around a clear optical intention. Temperature-monitored polishing keeps surface heat below 38°C at all times, achieving a 98.4+ Gloss Unit reflection where structure and sculpture become part of the same process.

Created with one person in mind.

The return of true tailor-made.

For Cardose, tailor-made means more than exterior polishing. It is a shared creative process in which the client works directly with our master detailers to restore Connolly leather, Alcantara, open-pore carbon, titanium exhausts, and magnesium wheel barrels.

The result is something more personal than a conventional detail: a Concours-ready one-off.

Ceramic architecture. Re-invented.

Lightning. Shielded.

Giving a permanent crystalline carapace to an automotive icon: Cardose GT Electric is our flagship 10H Graphene and solid-state polysilazane ceramic program. The study of unique optical depth and obsessive attention to detail are mixed with molecular nano-chemistry projected to the future.

Design meets molecular performance.

While being faithful to the pure soul of factory paintwork, Cardose combines multi-stage jeweling with a triple-layer Silicon Carbide (SiC) and Reduced Graphene Oxide (rGO) covalent matrix cured under short-wave infrared lamps at 65°C.

With a 118° water contact angle and a sliding angle below 12°, torrential rain, brake dust, and road grime sheet effortlessly off every exterior and interior surface.

Thermal & chemical carapace.

The GT Electric ceramic matrix consists of 92% active solid polysilazane cross-linked directly with your clear coat's molecular pores, reaching a dry film thickness of 4.2 microns and resisting chemical extremes from pH 2 to pH 13.

With a single commission, your vehicle is certified and warrantied for 7 full years of hydrophobic brilliance.

The company.

Cardose Automobili is a tailor-made handcraft surface engineering atelier whose mission is to collide the world-renowned Italian passion for elegance and aesthetics with a pure customer-driven approach.

Our team is a close-knit group of master detailers, CAD pattern engineers, and polymer specialists driven by a profound passion for the automotive world, who take care of every single process behind the preservation cycle of our clients' vehicles.

The imperative at Cardose is to join the highest level of technological and cleanroom solutions available on the market with the timeless design encapsulated in classic and modern automotive icons.

The values.

Rooted in the Italian handcrafting method, when a vehicle is under preparation in our cleanroom bays, we call our commissions "Operas", like those admirable in the finest art galleries in the world.

Every Opera is thought and engineered to be a proper piece of art on wheels, starting from ultrasonic micron mapping all the way to the invisible self-healing polymers protecting the carbon-fiber bodywork.
